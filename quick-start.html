ORRERY

Planetary Synthesizer · Quick Start · v0.300

The Orrery turns the real motions of the solar system — orbits, spins, moons — into sound. Every body is pitched and panned by its own orbit. Nothing is random: what you hear is the sky.

Make a sound in 30 seconds

  1. Open the file in Chrome or Edge, headphones on.
  2. Press Start. The Bells preset engages: the Sun's beam sweeps round and every planet and moon it touches rings.
  3. Drag the brass throttle to change how fast time runs — from 1:1 (real time) up to 100Myr a second. The hourglass ⧗ (a thousand years a day) is the sweet spot for bells.
  4. Click planet keys to mute and unmute them. ☉ Sun turns them all on — or all off.

The three presets

DroneEvery body holds a tone, fading in over a second. Opens the Scope view and sets the throttle to the hourglass.
ClockThe planets become clocks: each blips its own C on every planetary second — Mercury lowest, Pluto highest. Opens the planetary cards at real time.
BellsThe Sun's beam strikes each body as it passes. Click again to stop.
1stWith Bells: only the first body the beam hits in each system rings.

The top line

TRANSPORTStart / stop · Panic silences everything · Rec records a stereo WAV · now jumps to the real sky this moment · sync turns every planet to face the Sun (a downbeat).
VIEWClock the planetary instrument cards · Sky the solar system from above (opens here) · Scope a phosphor screen for each system.
SHOW ☀Click the sun to open extra display options (synth and FX racks, keys, names, moons, beams, scale, throttle). Hidden by default.

The planet keys

ClickMute / unmute that planet and its moons. Lit = audible.
Shift-clickSolo that planet. Shift-click it again to bring everyone back.
☉ SunAll on — or, when all are on, all off.
Major moons Minor moonsLit = audible. Major are the 19 round moons (our Moon, Io, Titan, Triton, Charon …); minor are all the rest.
InvertMirrors every pitch — slow outer orbits ring high, fast inner ones low — and turns the scopes negative.

Good to know

Systems are balanced: lonely Mercury sounds about as loud as Saturn with all its moons. Real-time (1:1) is genuinely slow — some events take minutes or years. Push the throttle to hear the slower clocks of the sky come in.

If it goes silent or strange, press Panic, then Start.
